# Terraria collaboration pages: recommendation only

Status: **recommendation for the owner. Nothing was changed, deleted, redirected or noindexed.**

## What exists
- 11 collaboration Pals, each with its own `/pals/<slug>/` page (they stay in the sitemap).
- Recipe counts (our data): Rainbow Slime 16, Eye of Cthulhu 10, Demon Eye 9, Enchanted Sword 7, Illuminant Slime 4, Purple Slime 4, Illuminant Bat 3 and others with 1 to 2.

## Risk
- Red, Blue, Purple and Illuminant Slime pages are very similar to each other (earlier text-similarity estimate about 0.85 to 0.87). Google may treat near-duplicates as one page.
- Search demand for individual collab Pals is small.
- The pages are useful for people who search a specific name, so thin-content removal is not clearly better.

## Options
1. **Keep as is (safe default).** Pal pages now carry data-driven blocks (special-recipe note, easiest catchable parents where applicable, data version). No action needed.
2. **Add a hub page** `/guide/terraria-collaboration-pals/` that compares all 11 and links to each page. Individual pages stay indexed. Lowest risk, mild benefit.
3. **Merge or noindex the slime variants.** Higher risk and needs owner approval. Not recommended until Search Console shows the pages are not indexed or get no impressions.

Recommendation: option 1 now, option 2 later if Search Console shows "Duplicate, Google chose different canonical" for these URLs. Do not delete or redirect anything without checking Search Console first.
